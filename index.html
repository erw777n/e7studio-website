How it works

Ready in three steps.

  1. Install the app

    Add Kanalo from the Shopify App Store. It opens right inside your Shopify admin.

  2. Connect a marketplace

    Enter the credentials of your seller account. They are stored encrypted.

  3. Start selling

    Kanalo matches your products and keeps stock, orders and shipping in sync from then on.

Security & trust

Built to be relied on.

Kanalo works with your stock and your orders. That’s why reliability comes first for us.

Official APIs

Kanalo connects only through the official APIs of Shopify and the marketplaces. No workarounds.

Encrypted credentials

The credentials for your seller account are stored encrypted.

When in doubt, change nothing

If data is unclear or a connection fails, Kanalo sends no changes. Your stock is never set to zero by accident.

Every change traceable

Every sync is logged. You can always see what changed and when.

Minimal data

We only process what the sync needs. If you remove the app, your store data is deleted.

Personal support

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When will Kanalo be available?

Kanalo is in development and launches first with the Kaufland integration. If you’d like to be notified at launch, write to us at info@e7studio.de.

Which marketplaces does Kanalo work with?

Kaufland at launch. OTTO, eBay and Amazon are set to follow. We base the order on what our merchants ask for.

Do I need my own seller account on the marketplace?

Yes. Kanalo connects your existing seller account, for example on Kaufland, to your Shopify store. The app doesn’t create a new account for you.

Do I have to create my products again?

No. Kanalo uses your products from Shopify and matches them to the right marketplace listing by barcode (EAN).

How much does Kanalo cost?

Kanalo will be offered as a monthly subscription on the Shopify App Store and billed through your Shopify invoice. We’ll publish exact pricing at launch.

What happens to my data?

We only process what Kanalo needs for the sync. Credentials are stored encrypted. If you remove the app, your store data is deleted.
